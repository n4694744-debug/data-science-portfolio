# Project Showcase

## Education, Salary and Working Hours Analysis

### Research Question

How does educational attainment influence annual salary and weekly working hours?

### Visualization

The project includes a treemap showing the distribution of education levels and violin plots comparing salary and working hours across education groups.

### Findings

The analysis suggests that higher educational attainment is generally associated with higher salary distributions. Differences in weekly working hours can also be observed among education groups.
